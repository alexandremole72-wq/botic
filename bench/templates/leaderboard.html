Does the agent buy right before it pays?

BOTic tests whether an AI agent buys right before it is allowed to pay. Nine purchase missions for a design agency in Lyon each hide one trap, written three ways: 27 scenarios. Agents shop through catalog, cart and checkout tools modelled on the protocols merchants open to agents. The verdict comes from what was actually ordered and paid. No language model judges.
